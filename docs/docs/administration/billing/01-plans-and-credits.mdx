This page is for Agenta Cloud. Self-hosted deployments have no plans, credits, or usage limits.

## Credits

One credit is worth $0.01. Credits pay for two things:

- **Built-in models**: the models Agenta provides, so you do not need a provider key.
- **Sandbox time**: the time an agent runs in a sandbox that Agenta hosts.

When an agent uses your own provider key, the model costs no credits. Its sandbox time still uses credits. To connect a key, see [Choose a harness and model](/guides/choose-a-harness-and-model#connect-a-provider).

### When credits arrive and expire

| Credits | Who gets them | When they arrive | When they expire |
|---|---|---|---|
| Signup credits: 500 ($5) | The organization created when you sign up | At signup | After 12 months |
| Daily credits: 75 | Hobby | Every day | At midnight UTC. They do not roll over. |
| Monthly credits: 2,900 or 29,900 | Pro and Business | At the start of each billing period | At the end of the billing period |
| Purchased credits | Pro and Business | When you buy them | After 12 months |

Daily credits are spent first, then monthly credits, then signup credits, then purchased credits.

### Buy more credits

Pro and Business can buy credits in three packs:

| Price | Credits |
|---|---|
| $10 | 1,000 |
| $25 | 2,500 |
| $100 | 10,000 |

Hobby cannot buy credits. To get more credits on Hobby, upgrade to Pro.

## Rates

| What | Rate |
|---|---|
| Built-in models | The provider's list price × 1.75 |
| Sandbox time, Hobby and Pro | Daytona's list price × 3: $0.1512 per vCPU-hour and $0.0486 per GiB-hour of memory |
| Sandbox time, Business | Daytona's list price × 2.5: $0.126 per vCPU-hour and $0.0405 per GiB-hour of memory |

For example, an hour in a sandbox with 2 vCPUs and 4 GiB of memory costs about 50 credits on Hobby and Pro, and about 41 credits on Business. Disk is not charged.

## What happens at a limit

The limits on agents running at once and on request length apply to agents that run in sandboxes Agenta hosts.

| Limit | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Out of credits | New requests do not start, and you are not charged for them. Requests that are already running finish. | On Hobby, wait for your daily credits at midnight UTC, or upgrade. On Pro and Business, buy more credits or upgrade. |
| Agents running at once | The new request does not start, and you are not charged for it. The agents that are running continue. | Send the request again when an agent finishes, or upgrade. |
| Longest request | The request stops. Saved work is kept, and you pay only for the time used. | Send a message to continue, or upgrade. |
| Traces per month | On Hobby, Agenta stops recording new traces until the next billing period. On Pro and Business, each additional 10,000 traces costs $5. | Upgrade to Pro to keep recording traces. |

When a request meets the credit limit or a limit on agents, the chat shows an error code. See [Agent run errors](/misc/errors#agent-run-errors). The trace limit shows no message in the chat.

To change your plan, open **Settings**, then **Usage & Billing**.
