## What you can do

Give every agent its own icon and color, then spot it quickly across navigation, chat, and automation pickers.

## How it works

Open an agent’s settings, choose an icon and color, and save the configuration. The identity appears wherever that agent is listed.

## Good to know

The reviewed implementation stores the choice locally; cross-browser persistence was not established.
