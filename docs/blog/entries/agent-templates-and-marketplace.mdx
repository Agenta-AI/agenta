<Summary>

You can now start from a ready-made agent instead of a blank one. Pick a template in the app or in the new [marketplace](https://agenta.ai/marketplace), and you get a working agent with its skills, files and the connections it needs.

Built something useful? Click **Save as template** and the agent packages itself so others can use it.

</Summary>

{/* truncate */}

## Start from a template

Open the template gallery on the home page, or browse the [marketplace](https://agenta.ai/marketplace) on our website. Each template has its own page. Click **Use it for free** and the app opens on the template's setup step.

Setup creates one agent with the template's instructions, skills and files, then asks you to connect the accounts it needs before the first task.

## Save your agent as a template

Click **Save as template** next to Publish. The agent writes a setup guide, leaves your secrets out, keeps automations off, checks the package and gives you a zip. You can share it, or submit it to the marketplace.
