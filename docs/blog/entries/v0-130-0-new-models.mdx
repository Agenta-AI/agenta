## New models

Your agents can use new models. To pick one, open an agent and choose the model in its model list.

## Built-in models

Built-in models are paid with your Agenta credits, like the other built-in models. You need no key of your own.

- Gemini 4 Hydrogen
- Gemini 4 Carbon
- Gemini 4 Neon

## Models for your own key or subscription

These models need your own provider key or subscription. Add it in **Settings** first.

- Claude Fable 5.2
- Claude Haiku 5.5
- GPT-6.2 Astra
- GPT-6.2 Sol
- GPT-6.1 Terra
- Mistral Large 4
