<Summary>

You can now use your ChatGPT subscription in Agenta. Click on the model selector, then configure models, and ChatGPT subscription. 

</Summary>

{/* truncate */}

Using a ChatGPT subscription previously required an operator to mount a login folder on a self-hosted runner. You can now connect the subscription from the app and choose it when configuring an agent.

## Connect your account

1. Open Settings, then AI providers, and choose Connect ChatGPT.
2. Copy the device code, open ChatGPT from the provided link, and approve the sign-in there.
3. Return to Agenta and wait for the provider card to show Connected.

Your sign-in credentials do not appear in the provider card.

## Choose it for an agent

Open the agent's Model section and select ChatGPT · Subscription. Pick one of the models listed for that connection and use the Pi runtime. You can use the connection for more than one agent, including agents running in a Daytona sandbox.

This device-code connection does not support the Codex runtime. A separately mounted Codex login is a different setup.

## If the login expires

Agenta refreshes the login during use. If ChatGPT rejects it, the connection shows Sign in needed and offers a new sign-in.

Your subscription's eligibility and usage limits still apply. Connecting it does not turn it into an unlimited model API plan.
