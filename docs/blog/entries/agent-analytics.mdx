Settings now has an **Analytics** tab, and Settings opens on it. You can see what your agents cost, how often they run and how often they fail, without digging through traces.

## What you see

Pick a range: the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or 90 days. The page charts:

- **Cost** over time
- **Runs** over time
- **Success rate**, with failed runs shown under the line
- **Tokens** by type
- **Failure rate by agent**, with the agents that fail most at the top

Ranges longer than your plan's trace retention are locked.

## Group and filter

Use **Group** to split cost, runs and tokens by agent or by model. Each agent or model keeps the same color in every chart. Use **Filter** to focus on some agents or models.

## Find the runs behind a number

Click **Explore** on a chart to open a breakdown by agent, model or tool, and the runs behind it. Switch between the most expensive runs and the failed ones, grouped by reason, and open any run's trace.
