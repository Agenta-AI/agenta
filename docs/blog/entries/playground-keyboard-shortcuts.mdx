<Summary>

You can now drive the playground from the keyboard. Press **?** to open the shortcut sheet.

Create, switch, rename and archive sessions, toggle panels, stop a run or approve a pending action without reaching for the mouse.

</Summary>

{/* truncate */}

## Open the shortcut sheet

Press **?** anywhere in the playground to see every shortcut. Buttons with a shortcut also show it in their tooltip.

## What you can do

- Create, close, switch, rename, archive and search sessions.
- Toggle the side panels.
- Stop a run.
- Approve a pending action.
