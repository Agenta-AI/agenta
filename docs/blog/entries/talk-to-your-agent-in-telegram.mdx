<Summary>

You can now chat with your agent in Telegram. Open the agent's Publish button, choose Telegram, and scan the QR code with your phone. That's the whole setup.

The chat keeps its memory across messages, so you can pick up a task from your phone where you left it.

## Connect in one scan

Open your agent, click **Publish** and choose Telegram. Scan the QR code, or tap the link, and press **Start** in Telegram. The chat is now connected to your agent. No bot token or BotFather needed.

## Use your own bot

If you want the agent to answer under your own bot name, choose **Use your own bot** and paste the token from BotFather.

## How the agent answers

A Telegram chat is one conversation, so the agent remembers earlier messages. It shows typing while it works and posts the answer as a new message. In groups, it only answers when it is addressed.
