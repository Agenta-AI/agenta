You can now send photos, documents, voice notes and videos to your agent in Slack, Telegram and WhatsApp. They reach the agent as attachments, so you can hand it a screenshot or a quick voice note from the chat app you already use.

</Summary>

Your agent can now receive files from the chat apps you connect it to. Send a screenshot, a PDF, a voice note or a short video, and the agent gets it as an attachment in its session.

## What you can send

- **Slack**: any file you share in the message.
- **Telegram**: photos, documents, voice notes, audio, videos and video messages, up to 20 MB each.
- **WhatsApp**: photos, documents, voice notes and videos.

Stickers, locations and contact cards aren't supported yet. The agent replies that it can't read them.

How much the agent can do with a file depends on its model. Images go straight to models that can see them. Other files are saved in the session, where the agent can open them with its tools.

To connect an agent, see [Connect Slack](/guides/connect-slack) and [Connect WhatsApp](/guides/connect-whatsapp).
