We cleaned up the app. Settings is easier to use, new features are easier to find, and help is one click away.

## A rebuilt Settings

Every Settings page now uses the same layout.

- **AI providers, integrations and channels** are catalogs of logos. Click **+** on one to connect it.
- **Lists** such as secrets, API keys, members and projects work on a phone, and you can rename most things in place.
- **Organizations**: switch between them from the list, create new ones, and transfer or delete them.
- **Members**: remove a member and rename yourself.

## See what's new

When we ship something new, the app opens a **What's New** window with a short video and a link to the changelog. It only shows once per release, and you can turn it off. Open it any time from the help menu.

Empty Agents, Automations, Skills and Sessions pages now explain what the page is for and suggest three templates to start from.

## Live chat with our team

On Agenta Cloud, the chat icon next to Help in the sidebar opens live chat with our team, without leaving the app.
