You can now share an agent conversation with your team. A session link opens the same chat, so your teammate can read the context and continue working with you.

## Share a conversation

Open the session menu and click Copy share link. You can do this from the session list or from the conversation itself. Send the link to a teammate who has access to the project.

They'll open that conversation with its existing messages. You don't need to copy the agent's replies into another chat to explain where things stand.

Session links work in both the classic and new apps. The link doesn't give someone project access or make the conversation public.
