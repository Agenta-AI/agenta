*Compare the best Manus alternatives for one-off tasks, persistent AI coworkers, team workflows, self-hosting, and business automation.*

[Manus](https://manus.im/) is a general AI agent for completing one-off tasks such as researching a topic, browsing websites, creating a presentation, analyzing files, or writing code. It is useful when you want to describe an outcome and receive a finished result without building a permanent workflow.

You may want a Manus alternative when the work should continue beyond one task. Some alternatives give you a broader AI workspace. Others let you create a coworker with lasting instructions, files, skills, schedules, team permissions, and access to the tools where your company already works. The right choice depends on whether you need a capable task agent, a reusable coworker, or a predictable automation.

## What Manus is best at

Manus is strongest when you have a task to complete now. You describe the result, provide any source material, and let the agent decide how to research, browse, analyze, or create the deliverable. This is a good fit for work that changes from request to request.

The trade-off appears when a useful task needs to become an ongoing capability. A persistent coworker needs more than a successful run. It may need lasting instructions, shared context, access controls, multiple communication channels, schedules, version history, and a clear way to improve its behavior over time. That is where the alternatives differ most.

## How to choose a Manus alternative

Use these three questions to narrow the list:

1. **Is this a task, a coworker, or a workflow?** Choose a general agent for changing one-off tasks, a coworker for ongoing interactive work, and a workflow builder for a stable process with known steps.
2. **How much control do you need?** A ready-made workspace is easier to start. An editable, self-hosted coworker gives you more control over models, instructions, tools, permissions, and data.
3. **Who needs to use and improve it?** Individual products optimize for personal productivity. Team products need shared context, private and public conversations, permissions, versioning, and visibility into cost and behavior.

## ChatGPT Work: best general Manus alternative for one-off work

[ChatGPT Work](https://chatgpt.com/) is the easiest starting point for people who already use ChatGPT. It is designed for longer, multi-step work and finished deliverables. It can research, analyze files, create documents, spreadsheets, presentations, reports, and websites, and use browser access where enabled.

The surrounding ChatGPT product makes Work broader than a single task window. Projects keep related chats, files, and instructions together. Scheduled Tasks can run work once, on a schedule, or when something changes. The desktop app can work with local files and applications with permission. Codex remains available for software development, so the same subscription can cover knowledge work and coding. Voice mode lets you direct Work and Codex by speaking.

![ChatGPT Work interface](/blog/best-manus-alternatives/chatgpt-work.png)

**Best for:** Individuals and teams that want one familiar product for research, files, presentations, spreadsheets, websites, browser tasks, and coding.

**Pricing:** Work is included with eligible paid ChatGPT plans. ChatGPT Plus starts at $20 per month. Pro costs $200 per month. Business plans add a team workspace and administrative controls.

**Pros:**

- Broad range of outputs, including documents, spreadsheets, presentations, reports, and websites
- Projects with reusable files and instructions
- Scheduled and triggered work
- Codex for software development
- Browser, computer, plugins, and connected-app access where supported
- Strong desktop voice experience

**Cons:**

- Limited to OpenAI models
- Hosted cloud work remains inside the OpenAI product and pricing model
- Less suitable than an open coworker platform when you need portable configuration or full infrastructure control

**Verdict:** Choose ChatGPT Work when you want a polished general agent that handles varied tasks with minimal setup. It is the closest fit for people who use Manus mainly to complete one job at a time.

## Genspark: best all-in-one AI workspace for small businesses

[Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/) packages many common business tasks into one approachable workspace. Its agents cover slides, spreadsheets, documents, dashboards, email, websites, design, content with AI images, meeting notes, code, video, and audio. This makes it useful for a small business that wants several AI tools without assembling a separate product for every output.

Genspark is organized around chats, files, tasks, and ready-made agents. AI Drive stores work in the same environment. SecondBrain provides shared memory across the workspace. Hubs separate projects or areas of work, while chats can contain files, tasks, and multiple threads. Genspark also offers workflows and templates, although the product places more emphasis on using its existing agents than on defining a deeply customized coworker from first principles.

![Genspark workspace](/blog/best-manus-alternatives/genspark.png)

**Best for:** Small and midsize businesses that want a simple AI workspace with many built-in production tools.

**Pricing:** Genspark has Free, Plus, and Pro plans with monthly credits. Plus begins with 10,000 monthly credits and Pro begins with 125,000. Its Team plan costs $30 per seat per month and includes 12,000 credits and 60 GB of AI Drive storage per seat.

**Pros:**

- Wide selection of task-specific agents
- Strong tools for slides, sheets, documents, design, media, meetings, and code
- AI Drive, project hubs, tasks, and shared memory
- Simple interface for small teams
- Access to models from several providers

**Cons:**

- Pricing has several plans, credit tiers, and add-ons
- Workflows are less prominent than the task-focused agents
- Limited control over the underlying instructions and behavior of built-in agents
- Lighter versioning, permissions, and multi-session controls than platforms built for customizable coworkers

**Verdict:** Choose Genspark when breadth and ease of use matter more than deep agent customization. It is one of the strongest options for a small business that wants AI to create many kinds of finished work from one workspace.

Claude Projects keep chats, files, project knowledge, and custom instructions together. Cowork can use connectors and skills, create artifacts, work through multi-step requests, and run scheduled tasks. **Pros:**

- Strong analysis, writing, and file handling
- Projects with instructions, files, and reusable context
- Skills, connectors, artifacts, browser access, and schedules
- Claude Code bundled with individual paid plans

- Fewer workspace surfaces and built-in production applications than Genspark
- Advanced configurations and integrations require more discovery and setup

**Verdict:** Choose Claude Cowork when your work is built around documents and project context. It is capable and focused, but it offers less model choice and platform flexibility than a configurable coworker workspace.

## Agenta: best for powerful, customizable AI coworkers

[Agenta](https://agenta.ai/) is an open-source workspace for building AI coworkers that keep working with you over time. Instead of using a fixed assistant, you can give each coworker its own instructions, skills, tools, files, schedules, channels, and permissions. The same coworker can handle interactive requests, join recurring workflows, and improve through feedback and versioned configuration.

Agenta is designed for teams that need both power and control. Coworkers can work through private or shared conversations and communicate through channels such as Slack and Telegram. They can connect to business tools through more than 1,000 integrations and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. MCP is a standard that lets an AI system use external tools and data sources. A technical team member can configure the coworker while a subject-matter expert improves it through normal feedback and use.

![Agenta coworker workspace](/blog/best-manus-alternatives/agenta.png)

Agenta also provides the operational controls that ongoing coworkers need. Teams can manage access, trace runs, track tokens and estimated costs, compare versions, organize many sessions, and inspect the models and tools involved. Coworkers run in isolated sandboxes with scoped access to files, computers, and secrets. You can use your own API keys, supported AI subscriptions, or self-hosted models, and you can self-host the platform and its configuration.

**Best for:** Startups and small technical teams that want powerful coworkers for complex ongoing work, with control over models, infrastructure, access, and improvement.

**Pricing:** Agenta is free and open source for self-hosting. Your costs depend on the infrastructure, models, API keys, or AI subscriptions you choose.

**Pros:**

- Deeply editable coworker instructions, skills, tools, files, and schedules
- Private and shared conversations with team permissions
- Slack, Telegram, and more than 1,000 tool integrations
- Model choice, bring-your-own-key support, and self-hosted models
- Tracing, analytics, token and cost visibility, feedback, and versioning
- Portable skills and open configuration
- Open-source, self-hostable platform with no required model vendor

**Cons:**

- Requires more setup than a fixed task agent
- Best suited to teams with a technical founder, operator, or teammate who can shape the initial configuration
- Its power is most useful for ongoing, complex work, not a single simple task

**Verdict:** Choose Agenta when you want to build coworkers rather than rent a fixed task agent. It is the strongest option on this list for teams that need persistent context, multiple channels, permissions, model freedom, observability, and a path to improve the coworker without losing previous versions.

## Lindy: best for prebuilt business assistance and visual workflows

[Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/) combines a business assistant with a visual flow editor, templates, skills, scheduled routines, approvals, and app integrations. It is built for everyday business work such as inbox management, meeting preparation and follow-up, research, support triage, and recurring updates.

Lindy is easier to start than a code-first automation system. Templates give teams working starting points, and the visual editor lets them connect steps into repeatable processes. It can work through Slack, iMessage, email, meetings, connected applications, and computer use. The trade-off is that the underlying agent behavior is less transparent and portable than a coworker defined through editable instructions, skills, tools, and versioned configuration.

![Lindy workflow interface](/blog/best-manus-alternatives/lindy.png)

**Best for:** Teams that want a ready-made assistant and visual workflows for common business processes.

**Pricing:** Lindy offers a seven-day trial. Plus costs $29.99 per user per month with 3,000 credits. Pro costs $99.99 with 15,000 credits, and Max costs $199.99 with 35,000 credits. Enterprise pricing is custom.

**Pros:**

- Fast setup for common inbox, meeting, research, and follow-up jobs
- Visual flow editor and a large template library
- Scheduled routines and human approvals
- Slack, iMessage, computer use, MCP, and thousands of integrations
- Choice among major AI models

**Cons:**

- Credit usage varies with task complexity
- Built-in assistants are less transparent than a fully editable agent package
- Deep customization and portability are not its main strengths

**Verdict:** Choose Lindy when you want a practical business assistant with ready-made routines and visual automation. Choose a more open coworker platform when the agent itself must be deeply customized, inspected, versioned, or moved between environments.

## OpenManus: best open-source framework for developers

[OpenManus](https://github.com/FoundationAgents/OpenManus) is an open-source framework for building and running Manus-style agents. It gives developers direct access to the code and lets them change models, prompts, tools, and execution behavior.

This is the most technical option on the list. It is a framework rather than a finished team workspace. Your team is responsible for setup, hosting, model access, security, collaboration, and maintenance.

![OpenManus GitHub repository](/blog/best-manus-alternatives/openmanus.png)

**Best for:** Developers who want an open-source starting point for a custom general agent.

**Pricing:** OpenManus is free and open source. Model, hosting, and engineering costs still apply.

**Pros:**

- Direct access to the codebase
- Flexible model, prompt, and tool configuration
- Control over the runtime
- Useful foundation for custom agent development

**Cons:**

- Requires Python, API keys, hosting, and hands-on engineering
- Does not provide a complete team coworker workspace out of the box
- Your team owns security, maintenance, permissions, and operational visibility

**Verdict:** Choose OpenManus when you want to engineer a custom Manus-style agent from a codebase. Choose a workspace product when non-developers also need to use, share, and improve the result.

## n8n: best for reliable, deterministic workflows

[n8n](https://n8n.io/) is a visual workflow automation platform that combines triggers, application integrations, code steps, approval points, and AI nodes. It is strongest when the process is known in advance and reliability matters more than open-ended planning.

n8n began as a more technical automation alternative to products such as Zapier and has expanded into AI workflows and agents. It is particularly useful for information technology operations, data movement, lead processing, support routing, and other processes where each step should be visible. Its large community, template library, and network of freelancers make it easier to find examples and implementation help.

![n8n workflow editor](/blog/best-manus-alternatives/n8n.png)

**Best for:** Technical operators and larger teams that need repeatable workflows with explicit steps and predictable execution.

**Pricing:** The self-hosted Community Edition is free. n8n Cloud starts at €20 per month billed annually for 2,500 workflow executions. Pro starts at €50 per month billed annually. The self-hosted Business plan starts at €667 per month billed annually, with a discounted startup plan available to eligible companies.

**Pros:**

- Reliable workflows with clear triggers, branches, and outputs
- Visual editor plus JavaScript, Python, JSON, API, and command-line options
- Large integration library, community, templates, and freelancer ecosystem
- Strong fit for information technology operations and data-heavy processes
- Cloud and self-hosted deployment
- Business and Enterprise controls for environments, versioning, scale, and governance

**Cons:**

- Steep learning curve for complex workflows
- Users often need to understand JSON, APIs, credentials, and data mapping
- Less natural for open-ended coworkers that decide how to approach changing work
- The Community Edition is source-available under n8n's Sustainable Use License, not open source under a standard Open Source Initiative license

**Verdict:** Choose n8n when you can define the process and need it to run consistently. It is excellent for deterministic automation, but building complex AI behavior can require a large node graph and substantial technical maintenance.

## Which Manus alternative should you choose?

- Choose **ChatGPT Work** for varied one-off tasks in a familiar, polished product.
- Choose **Genspark** for a simple AI workspace with many ready-made production tools.
- Choose **Claude Cowork** for document-heavy work and project context.
- Choose **Agenta** for powerful, persistent coworkers with deep customization, team control, model choice, and self-hosting.
- Choose **Lindy** for a ready-made business assistant with templates and visual workflows.
- Choose **OpenManus** for a developer-controlled Manus-style framework.
- Choose **n8n** for deterministic workflows with visible, repeatable steps.

The most important distinction is not which product has the longest feature list. It is whether you want an agent to finish a task, a coworker to stay with your team, or a workflow to execute the same process reliably.

## How to migrate from Manus

You do not need to recreate every task at once. Start with the work that repeats or benefits from shared context.

1. Separate one-off tasks from ongoing responsibilities and deterministic workflows.
2. Save the prompts, source files, examples, and output formats that already produce good results.
3. For each ongoing responsibility, document the instructions, tools, permissions, schedules, channels, and team members involved.
4. Test one real task in two alternatives using the same inputs and acceptance criteria.
5. Compare the result, supervision required, setup effort, cost, and whether the team can reuse and improve it.
6. Move the strongest workflow first, then repeat the process.

For a fixed automation, document every trigger, input, decision, approval, and output. For a coworker, preserve its purpose, context, working style, skills, tools, permissions, and examples of strong work.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best Manus alternative?

ChatGPT Work is the best starting point for varied one-off tasks. Genspark is the strongest all-in-one AI workspace for small businesses. Agenta is the best fit for teams that want to build powerful, persistent coworkers with full control over models, infrastructure, permissions, and configuration.

### What is the best Manus alternative for ongoing work?

Agenta is designed for ongoing coworkers with lasting instructions, tools, files, schedules, channels, feedback, and version history. Lindy is easier for ready-made business routines, while n8n is stronger for deterministic workflows.

### Is there a free Manus alternative?

Yes. Agenta and OpenManus are open source and can be self-hosted for free, although model and infrastructure costs still apply. n8n also offers a free self-hosted Community Edition under its Sustainable Use License. Genspark has a limited free plan.

### What is the best open-source Manus alternative?

OpenManus is a good choice for developers who want a code-first general-agent framework. Agenta is a better fit for teams that want a complete workspace for building, sharing, running, and improving AI coworkers.

### Can I self-host a Manus alternative?

Yes. Agenta, OpenManus, and n8n can run on your infrastructure. Agenta focuses on team coworkers, OpenManus on custom agent development, and n8n on repeatable workflows. n8n is source-available rather than open source under a standard Open Source Initiative license.

### Which Manus alternative is best for business automation?

Lindy is a practical choice for prebuilt business assistance and visual routines. n8n gives technical teams more control over deterministic workflows. Agenta is the better fit when the automation should also behave like an ongoing coworker with editable instructions, skills, tools, channels, permissions, and feedback.

### Which Manus alternative is best for research?

ChatGPT Work, Genspark, and Claude Cowork are strong ready-to-use choices. ChatGPT Work handles broad multi-step projects, Genspark combines research with many content tools, and Claude Cowork is particularly useful when research begins with a large collection of files.

### How should I compare Manus alternatives?

Run the same real task in each product with identical inputs and acceptance criteria. Compare the finished result, supervision required, setup effort, total cost, team access, and whether the work can become a reusable coworker or workflow.
